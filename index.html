01 / FRAME THE CONVERSATION

Executive overview

Ignite's recurring story is the move from AI assistance to governed, operational agents. Trusted context, measurable quality and explicit control boundaries matter as much as the model. Six themes connect the announcements:

  1. Copilot moves from answers to work

    Autopilot, Workspaces and Code extend assistance into persistent work and app creation. Frontier access and future release windows still apply.

  2. Enterprise context becomes reusable

    Microsoft IQ connects work context, business metrics, documents and the web. Individual integrations have different availability and licensing dependencies.

  3. From reports to applications

    Rayfin and Fabric apps reach GA. Power BI Pro app creation and Fabric HorizonDB remain preview, connecting analysis with controlled business actions.

  4. Agents need an operating model

    Agent 365, Entra, Defender, Intune and Purview extend visibility, identity, data protection and cost controls across cloud and local agents.

  5. Modernization meets resilience

    Azure Catalyst, database updates, Cobalt 200 and Chaos Studio address modernization and recovery. Preview operations agents add assistance, not blanket autonomy.

  6. Measure, improve, repeat

    Foundry connects evaluation, production insights, optimization and training. Success criteria and human approval remain essential before deploying a change.

How to read availability

One card represents one numbered source item, not one independently released feature. Mixed availability means the details matter. Future dates and contradictory source wording are called out rather than promoted to GA.

  • GA — explicitly generally available
  • Preview — access and release type vary
  • Frontier — program-gated access
  • Mixed — read per-feature evidence
  • Future / rolling out — not a universal availability claim

      • Specific per-feature status takes precedence over broad or contradictory introductory wording. Caveats are visible in the affected cards and exports.
      • Items may belong to multiple themes and stories. Selecting overlapping groups never duplicates an announcement.
